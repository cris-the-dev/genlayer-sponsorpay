Outage

Start a campaign
  1. 01Brief

    The brand names a creator, writes the requirements and escrows the fee. Nothing is paid until a post passes.

  2. 02Post

    The creator publishes with the campaign code and submits the link. Validators check every requirement; all met pays half.

  3. 03Stay up

    Anyone can recheck during the live period. Removed or edited out of compliance: the brand gets the rest back. Still up at the end: the creator does.
